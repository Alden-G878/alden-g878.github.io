- Investigating impact of existing implementations of path choice and current starved DCDL lines on range/resolution per unit area for PVT corners

- Developing techniques for determining the optimal combination of path choice and current starved implementations

- Verifying proposed techniques with brute force simulation on a small-scale model and directed simulation on a large-scale implementation
